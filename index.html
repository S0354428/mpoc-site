What MPOC is

MPOC connects Mercer students with prosthetics and orthotics learning, hands-on fabrication, cultural learning, clinical education, outreach, and developing research. Much of this work connects to Mercer On Mission Vietnam, and MPOC also runs its own club activities.

The Vietnam connection

MPOC is connected to Mercer On Mission Vietnam, a university program with its own structure, selection, and requirements. Club learning and fabrication may build toward practical use in the program where appropriate and approved.

MPOC membership does not guarantee selection for or participation in the program.
